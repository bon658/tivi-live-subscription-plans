## What should you look for in an online TV subscription?

The channel count gets all the attention because, well, 28,000 looks impressive sitting on a page.

But most people are not going to watch 28,000 channels. They want to find the channels they actually use without turning television into a part-time research project.

A useful online TV subscription should therefore be checked from a few practical angles:

* **Channel and VOD selection:** Does it have the live TV, sports, movies, and shows you actually care about?
* **Streaming quality:** 4K sounds great, but your device, connection, and the source all affect what you see.
* **Device support:** A service is much more useful when it works on the screens already sitting in your house.
* **Simultaneous connections:** One viewer and a whole family have very different requirements.
* **Trial or refund window:** A test period tells you much more about your own viewing experience than a giant feature list.
